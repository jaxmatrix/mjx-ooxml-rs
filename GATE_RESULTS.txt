THE WEAKEST PART OF THIS CHILD, STATED PLAINLY
----------------------------------------------
**The OMML typesetter's constants, and the stretchy delimiter above all.**

`mjx-text` parses no OpenType `MATH` table — a grep of that crate for `MATH` returns nothing at
all — so every number `crate::math` uses comes from somewhere other than the font: the two script
scale-downs from the `MATH` table's own documented defaults, the axis height from TeX's
`\fontdimen22` and MathML Core's fallback, the rule thickness from `\fontdimen8`, the fraction gaps
from *The TeXbook*'s Appendix G. Those are real external references and they are **not Word's
numbers**. What the gate asserts is therefore the *relationships* — a bar on an axis, a script at a
stated fraction, a delimiter that reaches its content, an array on one alignment axis — and not any
measurement.

The delimiter is worse than a constant. Without `MathVariants` there is no ladder of pre-drawn
sizes and no assembly, so a growing bracket is drawn by setting the glyph at a **larger size**,
which grows its stroke weight with its height. That is fine for the one-and-a-bit lines a fraction
or a sum needs and visibly wrong for a bracket around a ten-row matrix. It is marked `GUESS:` at
`grow_delimiter` and is the first thing the Windows sitting should look at.

Second weakest, and smaller: **a footnote mark under `w:numRestart="eachPage"`**. The mark's ordinal
is exact for `continuous` and `eachSect`, which are functions of document order; `eachPage` needs
the page the composition is an input to, so the body mark is numbered continuously while the note
area's own number restarts. Visibly inconsistent on such a document, declared at
`crate::model::note_mark`, and cheap to fix in a child that gives the per-page mark its own pass.

Third: **a numbered list that runs through a table** counts its in-cell items after the whole body,
because `DocumentFormatting::paragraphs` holds cell paragraphs after top-level ones — the order
MJXOFF-176 chose so a `w:sectPr` span stays numbered against the body walk. Declared at
`ListNumbering::read`.
